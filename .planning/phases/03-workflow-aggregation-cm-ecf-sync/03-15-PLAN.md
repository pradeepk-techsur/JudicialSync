<objective>
Ship the two queue screens into Phase 1's generic USWDS shell, and establish the Phase 3 nav aggregator
so the two later UI plans add screens without touching the shell again.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 and the first half of criterion 2 are user-visible claims — a user *sees*
their role-scoped tasks with oldest items flagged, *cannot* dismiss a legally significant one without a
rationale, and *sees* cross-module exceptions in one queue. CONTEXT locks the Work Queue as **the single
entry point**: an exception-backed task renders as a pointer that deep-links into the Exception Queue's
expanded triage row, which is what satisfies "one place to see what needs their attention" without
discarding the specialist triage surface Screen-13 designed.

Two contract rules bind every control here. Severity and age use **icon + text label + colour, never
colour alone** (Screen-13's explicit requirement and WCAG 1.4.1). And a control blocked on an unmet
condition is rendered **`aria-disabled` with permanent visible helper text, with a no-op handler** — the
02-UI-SPEC's resolution of its D-2/D-11 finding, because a `disabled` button is not focusable and its
tooltip never reaches a keyboard or screen-reader user. Phase 3 applies that pattern directly; it does
not re-derive it.

Output: the nav aggregator, the Work Queue, Screen-13's Exception Queue with its expanded triage row, and
Playwright coverage of both.
</objective>
