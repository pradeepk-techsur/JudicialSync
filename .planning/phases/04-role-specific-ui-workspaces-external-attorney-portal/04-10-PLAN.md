<objective>
Make the contract describe what Phase 4 actually built, split the portal's half into its own
document so the external bundle cannot carry internal route definitions, and register every place
Phase 4 departed from the specification.

Purpose: the project's binding rule is CONTEXT's — *"Deviating from spec is fine; deviating
**silently** is not — correct the contract and register it."* Phase 4 deviates in fifteen places,
one of which deliberately rewrites a security-relevant row of F12's error table and another of which
touches the audit hash function. Each needs the spec text, the replacement, the reason and the
corrected contract in one row a reviewer can read.

The split document is not bookkeeping. CONTEXT requires the external bundle to ship *"no internal
shell, no internal routes and no internal copy"*, and assurance proof 14 checks the built artifact.
A client generated from the full contract would embed every internal path as a typed operation.
Generating from a filtered document makes the guarantee structural rather than a thing to be careful
about.

Output: the extended OpenAPI emitter, two committed contracts, two regenerated clients, the
deviation register, the assumption register, the error-code catalog, and the drift test.
</objective>
