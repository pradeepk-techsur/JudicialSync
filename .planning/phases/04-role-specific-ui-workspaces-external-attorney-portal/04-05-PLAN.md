<objective>
Make every Phase 4 claim demonstrable by clicking: an attorney who can actually log in and see a
case, a sealed case they are genuinely a party to, a case they genuinely are not, a `jury_admin` who
lands on the unmapped-workspace page, and a user for every workspace and every panel.

Purpose: CONTEXT lists the seed additions explicitly — *"attorney principals with party-of-record
associations, a sealed case the attorney is party to (for proof 4), and a `jury_admin` user (for
proof 8)"* — and leaves *"seed content for demo attorney principals with party-of-record
associations"* to planning judgment. It also locks the resolution rule the seed must satisfy:
party-of-record comes *"strictly from party records synced through Phase 3's CM/ECF adapter"*, so the
seeded association has to be a `parties` row with a CM/ECF `external_id`, not a bespoke join table.

Two of the three Phase 4 success criteria are **rejection** claims. A rejection proof is only as
good as its negative control: proof 3 requires a 404 for a case that exists but the attorney is not
party to, **indistinguishable** from a 404 for a case that does not exist. Both of those need
fixtures, and one of them is a UUID that must deliberately never be inserted.

Output: `@judicialsync/fixtures` Phase 4 literals, the idempotent Phase 4 seed, the published portal
visibility policy, and the credential documentation.
</objective>
